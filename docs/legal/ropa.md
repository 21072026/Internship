## How to read the retention column

Every figure names the constant or the retention-registry key it comes from.
Registry keys are entries of `BUILT_IN_RETENTION_ENTRIES` in
`src/lib/retentionEntries.ts`, swept nightly; where an entry has a setting, the
default is the one in `SETTING_DEFAULTS` (`src/lib/settings.ts`) and an operator
or organisation may change it. **"Not yet enforced"** means no code deletes the
data by age: it lives as long as the account or record and goes with an erasure
(`src/lib/accountErasure.ts`).
