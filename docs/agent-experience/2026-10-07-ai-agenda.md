## 2026-10-07 — AI-personalised session agenda through the gate (#1845, PR follows)

- **Add an AI task in five files, not one.** The registry entry (`src/lib/ai/tasks.ts`), its rate-limit bucket (`src/lib/ai/limits.ts` — `AI_RATE_LIMITS.<task>` is what the route spreads into `enforceRateLimit`, so a missing key is a type error, which is the point), its cache TTL (`TASK_TTL_MS` in `src/lib/ai/artifacts.ts` — a task missing there is silently never cached, `findCachedArtifact` returns null), the audience table in the `aiGate.ts` header and the task-table row plus the degradation row in `docs/ai.md`. The route itself is the sixth.
- **Put the goal visibility rule inside the payload builder, not in the route.** `visibleGoals()` is pure, so `buildAgendaPayload({ goals, rel, viewer })` can apply it and the unit test proves a seat's PRIVATE goal never leaves — a route-level filter is untestable without Prisma and silently skipped by the next caller. The cache key then hashes the goals *actually sent*, not the viewer: the two seats share one stored answer when they may read the same set, and never read one built from a goal hidden from them.
- **"Degrade honestly" has a shape: 200 + the template + the reason.** A participant AI route must not answer a denial with a 4xx (the panel would show an error) and must not answer with nothing (the panel would show a blank). `{ proposals: templateFallback(guide, existing), source: 'template', reason }` keeps the templated agenda usable, lets the UI print one localized line per reason, and the only 403 left is the escalated admin's `feature_locked` (#1738). Assert the absence of `upgrade|yükselt|upgraden|feature_locked|AI_PACKAGE` over the serialized body, not a single field.
- **Accepting a proposal is a bulk write on the existing items endpoint**, not a new route: `{ texts: string[] }` next to `{ text }` with a `refine` that takes exactly one, `createMany` with consecutive `order`, one realtime signal. Reusing the endpoint reuses its relation rule, archived-relation 409 and capability gate, and `check:capability-writers` stays green without a second gate.
- **A new string with a role word costs three overlay entries; a role-neutral one costs nothing.** "The other side has not allowed AI on this pairing yet" instead of "the mentee has not consented" — the MARKETING leak ratchet (`check:i18n`) counts mentor/mentee/internship words per locale and fails on a new one.
