# 2026-10-29 — Teams removal via the Bot Framework JWT (#3322)

- A JWT check needs no library: `createPublicKey({ key: jwk, format: 'jwk' })` + `crypto.verify('RSA-SHA256', …)`
  is enough, and every claim check stays readable in one file. `jose` exists in node_modules only
  as a transitive dependency — do not import it.
- An e2e stub that SIGNS (`POST /__sign` on the mock) keeps the private key out of the repo and out
  of the spec, and can also sign with an unpublished key to prove a forged token is refused.
- Under `src/app/api/integrations/chat/`, add Teams/Slack-specific routes below `[platform]/` and
  404 the other platform — a static `teams/` sibling would shadow `[platform]` for every other
  `/chat/teams/*` path.
