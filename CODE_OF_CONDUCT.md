# Code of Conduct

**English** · [Türkçe](docs/code-of-conduct.tr.md) · [Deutsch](docs/code-of-conduct.de.md)

## Our pledge

Internship CRM exists to connect people at the start of their career with mentors
and companies willing to help them. That only works when everyone involved feels
safe and respected.

We — maintainers, contributors, and everyone using an Internship CRM instance —
pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for all, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, especially in the mentor ↔ mentee relationship,
  where one side is usually the more experienced and more powerful one.
- Give feedback about the work, not about the person. Assume good intent and ask
  before you conclude.
- Respect other people's privacy. Mentee profiles, CVs, interaction logs and
  contact details are shared with you for the programme, not for anything else
  (see [docs/DATA_ACCESS_POLICY.md](docs/DATA_ACCESS_POLICY.md)).
- Accept that decisions — about a placement, a PR, or a design — will not always
  go your way, and disagree gracefully when they don't.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances. Between a
  mentor and their mentee this is never acceptable, consent notwithstanding.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, and demeaning remarks about a person's
  background or ability.
- Publishing others' private information — email address, phone number, physical
  address, CV, immigration or health status — without their explicit permission.
- Using access granted by a role (admin, mentor, company contact) to obtain data
  you do not need for your task, or to pressure someone in a weaker position.
- Discriminating against a mentee in placement decisions on any of the grounds
  listed in the pledge above.

## Scope

This Code applies in every project space — the repository, issues, pull requests,
code review, and any deployed Internship CRM instance including its messages,
interaction logs and meetings — and whenever someone is representing the project
in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **ersahin@bcsit-gmbh.de**.

Reports are handled confidentially. Include what happened, when, who was
involved, and any evidence you can share (screenshots, links, message IDs) — but
report even if you have none. You may report on behalf of someone else, and you
may ask to stay anonymous towards the person reported. You will get an
acknowledgement within a few days.

Do not use public issues for reports. For technical security vulnerabilities use
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code, and for
removing, editing or rejecting contributions and content that violate it. They
will explain moderation decisions when it is appropriate to do so.

Depending on severity and repetition, consequences escalate:

1. **Private warning** — a written note explaining what was wrong and why.
2. **Warning with conditions** — no interaction with the people involved for a
   set period; a further violation leads to a ban.
3. **Temporary ban** — from the repository and/or the platform instance,
   including any active mentorship.
4. **Permanent ban** — for sustained violations, harassment of an individual, or
   abuse of the data or the power that a role grants.

A maintainer involved in an incident steps out of deciding it.
